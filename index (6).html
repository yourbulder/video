How to get your Google Drive video link

  1. Open drive.google.com and upload your video.
  2. Wait until Google Drive finishes processing the video.
  3. Right-click the video and choose Share → Share.
  4. Under General access, choose Anyone with the link and set it to Viewer.
  5. Click Copy link, then paste it below.

Only Google Drive file links are accepted. The video must be shared as “Anyone with the link – Viewer”.
