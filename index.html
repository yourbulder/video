How to add your video

  1. Upload your video to Google Drive and wait for processing to finish.
  2. Open Share settings and set General access to Anyone with the link — Viewer.
  3. Copy the Google Drive video link.
  4. Upload your thumbnail image to an image host and copy its direct image URL (not a page link).
  5. Paste both links below, add a title, and submit.

Paste a direct public image link that opens the image itself, not a hosting-site preview page. Use HTTPS when possible.
