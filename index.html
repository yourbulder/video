Videos are played from Google Drive. This website stores the shared links, not the video files.

Add a Google Drive video

Paste a public Google Drive link. The video will be added to the top of the library.

The link must be from drive.google.com and its sharing access must be set to “Anyone with the link – Viewer”.

How to upload and get the public link
  1. Open drive.google.com and sign in.
  2. Click New → File upload, choose your video, and wait for the upload to finish.
  3. Right-click the video and choose Share → Share.
  4. Under General access, change Restricted to Anyone with the link. Keep the role as Viewer.
  5. Click Copy link, then paste that link above.

Delete this video?

This removes the video from the shared library on every device. It does not delete the original file from Google Drive.
